aboutTitle: About Us
aboutContent: >-
  The VIITOR ACADEMIC Association is a non-profit initiative dedicated to
  supporting talented young people or those with outstanding academic results,
  coming from families with limited financial resources. We specifically address
  students from disadvantaged rural and urban areas, offering them moral and
  financial support to continue their higher education. We believe in the
  potential of every young person and work to transform it into real
  opportunities for academic and professional development.

  educational path, and successfully integrate into the labor market.
